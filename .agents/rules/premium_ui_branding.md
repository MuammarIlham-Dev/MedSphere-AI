# Premium "Lively Sapphire & Mint" Branding Guidelines

When asked to design or write implementation plans for a "multi-million dollar", "clean, calm, and cool", or "ultra-premium" brand experience with a "lively" feel, strictly enforce the following aesthetic rules:

1. **Surfaces & Shadows (Depth & Glass)**:
   - Use soft, diffused, large-radius box-shadows to add elegant floating depth (e.g., `0 10px 40px -10px rgba(0, 50, 150, 0.08)`).
   - Use subtle glassmorphism (translucency + backdrop blur + fine borders) for cards and overlays over off-white textured or gridded backgrounds.
   - Corners should be comfortably rounded (e.g., `rounded-2xl` or `rounded-3xl`) to feel approachable and modern.

2. **Typography & Macro-Whitespace**:
   - Use geometric or premium sans-serifs (like `Outfit`, `Inter`, or `Playfair Display`) with tight kerning for headers.
   - Enforce "macro-whitespace" — use massive padding (e.g., 48px to 96px, `p-12`, `p-16`) between sections and within cards to create a breathable, gallery-like layout.

3. **Color Palette (Sapphire Blue & Vivid Mint)**:
   - Anchor the design in off-white technical backgrounds (e.g., `#F8FAFC` with grid patterns).
   - Use deep Sapphire Blue for primary text, major headings, and primary buttons (e.g., `#0F172A` to deep rich blues).
   - Inject life and energy using Vivid Mint / Vibrant Teal (e.g., `#06b6d4`, `#10b981`) for accents, charts, success states, and focus rings.

4. **Animations & Micro-interactions**:
   - Use smooth, floating animations with a slight bouncy/spring physics (e.g., 400ms to 600ms easing) to feel tactile, interactive, and lively.
   - Add hover effects that slightly lift elements and increase shadow blur to encourage engagement.
