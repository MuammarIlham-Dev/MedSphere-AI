3. Micro-Interactions & UX Requirements
Haptic & Motion Design:

Primary button taps provide light impact haptic feedback (UIImpactFeedbackGenerator(style: .light)).

Card transitions utilize spring physics (damping: 0.8, response: 0.35s).

Category chips horizontally glide with fluid momentum scrolling and auto-centering on selection.

Accessibility Targets:

All interactive touch targets must meet or exceed 44 x 44 pt minimum dimensions.

Text contrast ratios must strictly respect WCAG AA: Minimum 4.5:1 for standard body text against background, and 3:1 for graphical interface components.

Semantic labels on all icon-only buttons (Back, Bookmark, Notifications, Emergency Heart).
