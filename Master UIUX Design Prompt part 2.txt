Comprehensive UI/UX Design System & Master Brand Guidelines
1. Brand Core, Visual Identity & Design Tokens
Brand Archetype & Philosophy
Archetype: The Caregiver + The Sage (Clinical Authority meets Deep Human Empathy).

Tone of Voice: Authoritative, calming, precise, unhurried, and unambiguous.

Vigour & Contrast: Deep arterial crimsons and burgundy hues establish specialized cardiac competence without the alarming fluorescence of emergency alerts. Surfaces maintain a sterile yet warm porcelain cleanliness.

2. Layout, Viewports & Structural Containers
Spatial System
Base Grid: 8px strict grid system (padding and margins follow 8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px).

Micro-Grid: 4px micro-adjustments for badge insets, avatar overlapping, and icon spacers.

Mobile Viewport: 390 x 844 pt (iPhone native standard) with 42px corner radius and Dynamic Island clearance.

Desktop / Tablet Extension: 12-column responsive fluid grid (max-width 1280px, 24px gutters, 32px margins).

4. Animations, Effects & Micro-Interactions
A. Animation Physics & Curves
Press & Release Physics: Scale compression to 0.97 on touch-down over 120ms ease-out; spring rebound over 250ms with damping ratio 0.75.

Page Transitions: Shared-element hero transition for the doctor portrait and appointment card (350ms, cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)).

Skeleton Loader: Left-to-right linear gradient shimmer (rgba(255,255,255,0) to rgba(255,255,255,0.6) to rgba(255,255,255,0)) sweeping every 1.4s.

B. ECG & Cardiac Heartbeat Pulse
ECG Waveform Trace: SVG stroke dasharray infinitely scrolling leftward over 1800ms linear.

3D Heart Ambient Beat: Subtle scale pulse between 1.00 and 1.03 following a biphasic systolic/diastolic rhythm (duration 900ms per cycle).

C. Haptic Feedback Profile
Primary CTA Tap: Light Impact Haptic (UIImpactFeedbackGenerator(style: .medium)).

Selection Toggles (Filter Chips, Visit Types): Selection Tick (UISelectionFeedbackGenerator).

Emergency Cardiac Trigger: Distinctive Double Pulse Haptic.

5. Full-Screen UI/UX Itinerary
Screen 1 (Onboarding): Atmospheric dark-burgundy viewport anchored by the brand mark, floating anatomical heart with active ECG, bold value messaging, and dual CTA dock.

Screen 2 (Discovery Dashboard): Warm white surface, patient identity cluster, category filtering, solid crimson schedule hero card, diagnostic center directory, and persistent 4-node navigation dock.

Screen 3 (Consultation Detail): Physician credential overview, two-node dashed timeline stepper, interactive crimson visit-type selector, 4-column procedure roadmap, and institutional reassurance footer strip.

Screen 4 (Desktop / Tablet Extension): Responsive 12-column outpatient booking console uniting the collapsible sidebar, omni-search app bar, interactive calendar grid, and regulatory institutional footer.
